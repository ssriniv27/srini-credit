# Srini Credit

Srini Credit is a corporate credit-analysis application that evaluates a public company’s financial condition and produces a proprietary credit score out of 100.

The application analyzes liquidity, leverage, profitability, cash flow, market risk, and historical financial trends. It then assigns a credit tier, identifies strengths and warning signals, and generates a downloadable analyst report.

## Live Application

Try Srini Credit here:

[Open the Srini Credit Dashboard](https://srinicredit.streamlit.app/)
## Features

- Company analysis by stock ticker
- Srini Credit score from 0 to 100
- Credit-tier classification
- Lending recommendation
- Liquidity, leverage, profitability, cash-flow, and market-risk scores
- Historical revenue, EBITDA, net-income, cash-flow, debt, and margin trends
- Financial warning signals
- Historical stock-price visualization
- Detailed analyst memo
- Downloadable TXT and PDF reports
- Interactive Streamlit dashboard

## Technology
